# Release policy checklist — not approval guarantee
Policies can change. Captain must re-check official sources immediately before release. Choosing Education or omitting words does NOT exempt an app from policies based on its actual function. Disclaimer is not immunity. Do not promise no rejection or that IAP solves a news-policy issue.

## Government information
Independent aggregators must clearly disclose non-affiliation and easy-to-see official sources in app and store listing. Government app declaration must truthfully answer whether developed by/on behalf of government; Karmopath currently is not. Government-agency apps have organization-account requirements; no blanket assertion that the word Govt bans personal developers. Never claim official status, authorization or guaranteed recruitment. No emblems/official badges; link out to official applications rather than collecting forms/payment/documents.
Sources:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9514050
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890

Display: “Karmopath is an independent information platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government entity. Verify eligibility and dates in the original official notice before applying.” Provide equivalent Bengali. Include developer contact and correction channel; owner email must be supplied before launch, never invented.

## News scope and intellectual property
Review actual content against News & Magazine requirements and complete any applicable declaration; Education listing is not a loophole. No copied newspaper PDFs, competitor summaries or unlicensed logos. Link to original notices; don't assume all government/public text is copyright-free. Record origin and human review.
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9935326

## AdMob / privacy
Test ad IDs only during development. Ads must be distinct, not next to Apply/download taps, never disguised as jobs, no launch/back-exit forced ads. MVP placement: clearly labeled, non-obstructive list banner after content; no interstitials initially. App remains useful with no ads/network. Complete Data safety from real SDK behavior; privacy policy in app/store; region-appropriate consent before ad requests, determine audience honestly, no child targeting by default. Review notification permission and Android scheduling restrictions. No unnecessary storage/location/contact permissions.
Verify current app ownership and app-ads.txt requirements with official AdMob docs; free GitHub Pages developer-site compatibility must be verified, not promised. Owner configures real publisher/app IDs and deployment. No production keys bundled.
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9363762

## Submission blockers
Verify current target SDK deadline, account type/identity verification and applicable personal-account testing requirements in Play Console (12 testers/14 continuous days may apply). Test native functionality, accessibility, offline/error flows and correction links. Prepare screenshots with real UI, privacy policy, support contact, content rating and accurate ads declaration. Do not upload until captain review and owner authorization. No guaranteed revenue, acceptance or fixed appeal allowance assumed.
